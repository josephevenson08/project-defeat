# The Burning Crusade: look, motion, and TBC Anniversary (research question A)

Researched 2026-10-04 for Project Defeat's `round4/motion-3d-portal.html` direction. Every factual
sentence ends with source IDs from `sources-a.md`. Tiers: **P** primary, **R** reputable secondary,
**W** weak. A claim backed only by weak sources is labelled *(weak only)*; anything I could not
confirm is labelled *unverified*. Lines marked **Interpretation** are my own design reading, not facts.
Blizzard art is described in words only; no images were downloaded or embedded.

## 0. Status check first: Phase 2 is no longer the current phase

- TBC Classic Anniversary Phase 2, "Overlords of Outland", opened Serpentshrine Cavern and Tempest Keep: The Eye on 14 May 2026. [TA3][TA4][TA13]
- Phase 3 (Black Temple and Battle for Mount Hyjal) went live on 27 August 2026. [TA6][TA7][TA5]
- So, as of 2026-10-04, a "Phase 2" planner describes the previous phase, not the live one. [TA6][TA7]
- A community wiki lists Phase 3.5 (Zul'Aman) and Phase 4 (Sunwell) for autumn 2026; *unverified*, (weak only). [TA18]
- **Interpretation:** the SSC/TK motion theme still fits gear planning for T5, but the copy should not say "current phase" without a date.

## 1. TBC's visual identity

### 1.1 The Dark Portal and the login screen

- The original Burning Crusade released on 16 January 2007 in Europe and North America. [TA45] *(weak only; date widely repeated, not re-checked against a 2007 Blizzard release)*
- The Dark Portal was the subject of TBC's login screen. [TA19][TA20][TA46]
- That screen ran from January 2007 until Wrath of the Lich King in November 2008. [TA19] *(weak only)*
- PCGamesN (May 2026) calls that screen possibly the game's most iconic art, and describes the portal flanked by leering statues under a dragon-like gargoyle. [TA20]
- The wiki records "The Burning Legion" as the login music for that screen. [TA19] *(weak only)*
- The portal is a rectangular, stone-carved frame around a dimensional rift. [TA22] *(weak only)*
- Its energy is described as blue when dormant and green once the Legion reopened it, which is the colour of the TBC era. [TA22][TA21] *(weak only)*
- The TBC intro cinematic shows the portal fading from blue to green as it reactivates, then cuts to a green-tinted Outland sky. [TA21] *(weak only)*
- The Outland side (the Stair of Destiny in Hellfire Peninsula) is larger and more ornate than the Azeroth side. [TA22] *(weak only)*
- A Burning Crusade Classic login screen exists on the Classic progression branch. [TA19] *(weak only)* Whether TBC Anniversary uses a new or reused login screen is *unverified* (no source found).

### 1.2 Outland's sky: the Twisting Nether, floating rock, Netherstorm

- Outland is the shattered remains of Draenor, torn apart by Ner'zhul's portals. [TA23] *(weak only)*
- Blizzard's own Anniversary copy leans on the same idea, describing Outland's "shattered skies and torn lands". [TA1]
- Blizzard's Phase 2 copy also calls it "the shattered world of Outland". [TA4]
- The wiki describes the Twisting Nether as visible in Outland's sky as an aurora-like effect, with prismatic clouds and twisting ribbons of energy. [TA25][TA23] *(weak only)*
- It also notes several large celestial bodies hang in the Outland sky. [TA23] *(weak only)*
- Netherstorm is a set of floating islands and thousands of smaller rock fragments drifting in the Nether. [TA24] *(weak only)*
- Its sky is described as violet haze cut by arcing magical lightning and bands of energy. [TA24] *(weak only)*
- Five manaforges siphon Nether energy and feed Tempest Keep through conduits. [TA24] *(weak only)*
- Ethereal-built domes keep pockets of green life alive inside the barren zone. [TA24] *(weak only)*
- Contemporary reviewers praised Outland's scenery; Wikipedia summarises reviews calling it "occasionally breathtaking". [TA45] *(weak only; tertiary summary of reviews)*

### 1.3 Fel green and Hellfire Peninsula

- Fel magic is drawn as a glowing green, and felfire is a green flame that burns hotter than ordinary fire. [TA38] *(weak only)*
- Hellfire Peninsula, the first zone through the portal, is a red, barren, hard-packed land under a blood-red sky. [TA37] *(weak only)*
- Its landmarks include Hellfire Citadel, towering Fel Reavers, and floating islands at the shattered edges. [TA37] *(weak only)*
- The Anniversary launch trailer recreates running from the Fel Reaver and killing early boars in Hellfire. [TA17][TA16] *(weak only)*
- The TBC-era logo recoloured the World of Warcraft mark from blue to fel green and added the expansion name beneath it. [TA43] *(weak only; logo detail otherwise unverified)*

### 1.4 Naaru light and the Draenei / Aldor crystal look

- Blizzard's in-game quest text for meeting A'dal describes light filling the senses and "melodic chimes" ringing in the player's head. [TA35]
- So the naaru are written as light plus sound: a glow that intensifies as you approach, with chiming. [TA35][TA36]
- The wiki describes naaru bodies as crystalline segments rotating around a central core of Light. [TA36] *(weak only)*
- Naaru built Tempest Keep, a dimensional fortress able to travel through the Twisting Nether. [TA26][TA27] *(weak only)*
- The Exodar, the draenei capital, is one of Tempest Keep's satellite vessels. [TA26] *(weak only)*
- Draenei are described with blue-to-violet skin, glowing eyes, and a design language of crystals and sculpted armour. [TA40] *(weak only)*
- The Aldor are draenei priests who serve the naaru A'dal from Aldor Rise in Shattrath. [TA42] *(weak only)*
- The Anniversary trailer includes seeing A'dal in Shattrath as one of its nostalgic beats. [TA17][TA16] *(weak only)*
- Specific Aldor faction colours (gold and light blue in one wiki summary) are *unverified*. [TA42]

### 1.5 Blood Elf / Scryer arcane: crimson, gold, phoenix, and fel-green eyes

- Blood elves are associated with crimson and gold, and the phoenix is their emblem. [TA39] *(weak only)*
- In TBC-era lore, many blood elves have fel-green eyes from living near demonic energy used to rebuild Silvermoon. [TA39][TA38] *(weak only)*
- The Scryers are blood elves who left Kael'thas to serve the naaru, led from Scryer's Tier in Shattrath. [TA41] *(weak only)*
- The Scryers' symbol is an eye. [TA41] *(weak only)* Their exact tabard colours are *unverified*.
- Kael'thas's phoenix Al'ar is the first boss of The Eye, which ties the blood elf phoenix to Phase 2. [TA32][TA27] *(weak only)*

### 1.6 Interface chrome, tooltips, item colours, typography

- Item quality is shown by name colour: poor #9d9d9d, common #ffffff, uncommon #1eff00, rare #0070dd, epic #a335ee, legendary #ff8000. [TA44] *(weak only; matches widely used values but not checked against client code)*
- The prototype currently uses its own softened quality colours (for example epic `#c08cff`, rare `#4ba3ff`), not these values. (Observation of `round4/motion-3d-portal.html`, read 2026-10-04.)
- The default tooltip's exact background and border colours could not be sourced; *unverified*. (See `log-a.md`, Q31 and Q32.)
- No source found on the TBC logo's typeface. *Unverified*; I did not fill this from memory.

## 2. Phase 2 raids

### 2.1 Serpentshrine Cavern (Coilfang Reservoir, Zangarmarsh)

- Blizzard calls SSC Lady Vashj's "watery lair" in Coilfang Reservoir, Zangarmarsh, with six bosses. [TA3]
- Blizzard names Vashj "the Sea Witch" and one of Illidan's lieutenants. [TA3]
- In lore, Vashj's naga use the reservoir to drain Zangarmarsh and control Outland's water. [TA28] *(weak only)*
- The reservoir is described as huge caverns with giant pipes and pistons, powered by crystal capacitors. [TA28] *(weak only)*
- Its entrance is underwater, and the caverns inside are only partly flooded. [TA28] *(weak only)*
- SSC's main cavern is a set of large bridges and platforms over water. [TA29] *(weak only)*
- Hydross the Unstable stays in a pure water form between two beams of energy and turns to a poison form when pulled out of them. [TA30] *(weak only)*
- The Lurker Below is started by fishing it out of its pool, not by a normal pull. [TA29] *(weak only)*
- Leotheras the Blind shifts into a demon form and splits off inner demons. [TA29] *(weak only)*
- Vashj's phase 2 is a shield held up by four generators, which raiders switch off with Tainted Cores taken from Tainted Elementals. [TA31] *(weak only)*
- Her phase 3 adds Toxic Sporebats that leave poison clouds. [TA31] *(weak only)*

### 2.2 Tempest Keep: The Eye (Netherstorm)

- Blizzard calls Tempest Keep a "crystalline fortress" in Netherstorm ruled by Kael'thas, the Sun King. [TA3]
- The Eye has four bosses: Void Reaver, Al'ar, High Astromancer Solarian and Kael'thas. [TA3][TA13]
- Tempest Keep was built by the naaru and later seized by Kael'thas and his blood elves. [TA26] *(weak only)*
- The Eye's wings are the Phoenix Hall, the Crucible, the Solarium and Tempest Bridge. [TA27] *(weak only)*
- Al'ar is a fire phoenix that moves between platforms, dive-bombs, and is reborn from its ashes. [TA32] *(weak only)*
- Solarian's adds arrive through circular light portals, and she turns into a giant voidwalker near death. [TA33] *(weak only)*
- Kael'thas summons seven legendary weapons that float around the platform in his second phase. [TA34] *(weak only)*
- In the last phase the arena's windows shatter, Gravity Lapse lifts the raid into the air, and black Nether Vapor clouds drift through the room. [TA34] *(weak only)*
- Kael'thas also casts Shock Barrier and Pyroblast, and phoenixes leave eggs that rehatch if not killed. [TA34] *(weak only)*
- A "space-like" skybox for The Eye was not described in any source I could load; it is *unverified*. The closest sourced facts are Tempest Keep's ability to travel through the Nether and the shattering windows. [TA26][TA34]

## 3. TBC Classic Anniversary (2025 to 2026)

### 3.1 Timeline

- When the fresh Classic anniversary realms opened on 21 November 2024, Blizzard Watch reported they would move into TBC Classic around the end of 2025 or start of 2026. [TA11] *(background; pre-dates the Anniversary launch)*
- On 25 November 2025 Blizzard opened free transfers and said non-Hardcore anniversary realms would convert to TBC Classic anniversary realms. [TA10]
- The release date was announced on 7 January 2026. [TA8][TA9]
- The pre-patch went live on 13 January 2026. [TA9][TA1]
- The expansion launched on 5 February 2026 at 3 pm Pacific. [TA2][TA8][TA9]
- Arena Season 1 began 17 February, and Karazhan, Gruul's Lair and Magtheridon's Lair opened 19 February 2026. [TA1][TA2]
- Blizzard announced Phase 2's 14 May date in advance [TA4]; the announcement's day (around 15 April 2026) comes only from a fan site's report date. [TA14] *(weak only)*
- Phase 2 ("Overlords of Outland") launched 14 May 2026 with SSC, The Eye, Ogri'la, Sha'tari Skyguard and Arena Season 2. [TA3][TA4][TA13]
- Phase 3 launched 27 August 2026. [TA6][TA7][TA5]

### 3.2 What Blizzard published and how it looks or reads

- Blizzard's launch article (one article ID, retitled from pre-patch to launch to "now live") offered key art as a 3840x2160 wallpaper and "colour and ink" artwork in several sizes. [TA1]
- The wiki lists Anniversary launch key art plus standard and ink poster versions. [TA18] *(weak only)*
- I did not view the key art or posters, so I cannot describe their composition; *unverified*.
- Blizzard's copy repeats a "shattered / torn" world motif ("shattered skies and torn lands"; "shattered world of Outland"). [TA1][TA4]
- Blizzard's Phase 2 copy frames the raids as Illidan's lieutenants: a crystalline fortress and a watery lair. [TA3][TA4]
- Wowhead published Blizzard's Anniversary launch trailer under the title "Blizzard Presents"; the article body did not load. [TA15]
- Two weak reports say the trailer was made with Hurricane, a known WoW video creator, and looks more realistic than usual WoW trailers. [TA16][TA17] *(weak only)*
- They list its beats as crossing the Dark Portal, Hellfire boars, fleeing the Fel Reaver, and A'dal in Shattrath. [TA17] *(weak only)*
- Some MMO-Champion commenters disliked its use of modern-client character models. [TA16] *(weak only; reader comments)*
- Blizzard's shop sells an "Anniversary Dark Portal Pack" (seen in search results); its contents could not be loaded behind a sign-in wall; *unverified*. (log-a.md, Q35)
- I found no source describing an Anniversary-specific login screen or Battle.net launcher art; *unverified*.

## 4. Motion patterns that read as "TBC"

Each line gives the sourced fact, then an **Interpretation** for animation. The interpretations are
design suggestions, not claims about Blizzard's work.

1. **Portal ignition, blue to green.** The Dark Portal goes from blue (dormant) to green (reopened), and the cinematic shows that fade. [TA21][TA22] *(weak only)*
   **Interpretation:** an intro where a ring or frame starts cool blue and crossfades to fel green over about one second, once.
2. **Fel fire and embers.** Felfire is green and hotter than normal fire, and Hellfire's sky is blood red. [TA38][TA37] *(weak only)*
   **Interpretation:** slow upward green or red-orange embers with additive glow, for a Phase 1 or Hellfire theme.
3. **Nether sky shimmer.** The Nether shows aurora-like ribbons and prismatic clouds; Netherstorm has violet haze, arcing lightning and energy bands. [TA25][TA24] *(weak only)*
   **Interpretation:** slow-moving violet and teal ribbons behind the scene, with rare brief lightning flickers, for a Tempest Keep theme.
4. **Floating rock.** Netherstorm and Hellfire have floating islands and drifting fragments. [TA24][TA37] *(weak only)*
   **Interpretation:** a few low-poly rocks that bob and rotate very slowly, like the existing crystal shards.
5. **Naaru pulse.** Approaching A'dal, light intensifies and chimes ring. [TA35] Naaru bodies are rotating crystal segments around a core. [TA36] *(weak only)*
   **Interpretation:** a crystal whose orbiting shards rotate steadily, with a soft brightness pulse on events (a "chime"), optionally paired with a quiet sound only after user consent.
6. **Water and machinery (SSC).** SSC is bridges over water inside pipe-filled caverns with crystal capacitors. [TA28][TA29] *(weak only)*
   **Interpretation:** the prototype's water plane fits; caustic light and slow pump-like pulses would read as Coilfang. Caustics themselves are not described in any source.
7. **Purity versus taint (Hydross, Vashj).** Hydross switches between pure water and poison forms; Vashj's shield falls to Tainted Cores. [TA30][TA31] *(weak only)*
   **Interpretation:** a two-state colour shift (clear teal to sickly green) to signal a state change, such as a gear swap.
8. **Phoenix and rebirth (Al'ar, Kael'thas).** Al'ar is reborn from ashes; phoenix eggs rehatch. [TA32][TA34] *(weak only)*
   **Interpretation:** a burst of gold-orange sparks that settle and re-form, for "reset" or "restore" actions.
9. **Gravity lapse and shattered glass (Kael'thas).** Windows shatter and the raid floats. [TA34] *(weak only)*
   **Interpretation:** a once-only "lift" where cards rise slightly and particles slow, used sparingly for a big moment.
10. **Arcane portals (Solarian).** Adds emerge from circular light portals. [TA33] *(weak only)*
    **Interpretation:** small expanding rings as entry transitions for panels.

Arcane runes as a motion motif are not described in any source I loaded; *unverified*.
