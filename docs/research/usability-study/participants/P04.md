## Their plan, written before the visit

### Plan — P04 (Leo)

#### What I usually do on a new site
- I don't read, I scan. If a page is a wall of text I'm already gone.
- I click the biggest/shiniest thing first and see what happens — I learn by poking, not by reading instructions.
- I check if it "gets" me fast: does it look legit, does it load fast, is it obvious what to do first.
- If I see words I don't know I just skip past them unless they're blocking me from doing the thing I want.
- I bounce fast if I feel lost for more than a few seconds — there's always another tab to open.
- I look for anything that feels like "my stuff" (login, my character, my progress) since that's usually the fun part.

#### What I'm hoping to get out of this visit
Some YouTuber's comment section linked this site and people said it helps you get better at your character. I have a level 58 Paladin and I want to know what I should actually be doing to get stronger — like what to aim for next, gear-wise or whatever. I don't know what half the raid names or shorthand mean yet so hopefully it's not too confusing.

## Their report, written after the visit

### P04: Leo

#### In one paragraph
Project Defeat is a slick, dark-fantasy-themed planning tool for World of Warcraft TBC Classic raiders — build a character, get a best-in-slot gear list, run a rough damage estimate, see spec tier lists, and browse raid loot. It looks professional and some parts (the tier list, the one-click "equip" button) answered my "what should I do to get stronger" question fast and clearly. But it's built assuming you already raid at max level and know the lingo — I'm 58, and most of the paragraph text (raid attunement chains, boss names, ability names, stat-weight talk) went straight over my head.

#### First impression
Big splash art, gold trim, "TBC CLASSIC · PHASE 2," "For the Alliance and for the Horde" (step 1). It reads as a serious tool for people already deep into the game — the five cards (Character Planner, Raid Composition, Spec Tier Lists, Raids, Professions) all talk about "Phase 2 rankings," "10 or 25-player roster," and named raids. I guessed right: this is for raiders optimizing a max-level character, not someone still leveling like me. It looks legit, not like a cheap fan wiki.

#### What I came for, and what I actually did
I wanted to know what to do to get stronger on my Paladin. I built a Dwarf Retribution Paladin through the wizard (steps 2-10), poked at the gear screen (steps 11-22), found the Ranked Gear list and used its Equip button (steps 23-24), ran the Simulation (steps 25-27), checked the Spec Tier List (steps 28-29), browsed Raids and a boss's loot (steps 30-34), and glanced at Professions (step 35).

#### Where I got stuck or confused
- Steps 11-14: I opened the Head gear slot, clicked "[138] Crown of the Sun" in the list, even pressed Enter — the detail panel and the actual head slot on my character sheet both kept saying "Empty." I closed the popup thinking it just didn't work.
- Steps 15-18: I reopened it and tried to use it like a normal dropdown; the tool itself even told me the field "is not a real select element" when I tried the standard way. Had to click a second time directly on a different row before anything registered (step 19), then it worked (step 20).
- Step 27: after running the simulation I got "ESTIMATED DPS 28.2" — no idea if that's good, bad, or what it's out of. No bar, no comparison, nothing.
- Step 32: the Karazhan write-up throws out "Moroes → Opera → The Curator → Chess → Prince Malchezaar, with Attumen, Maiden, Illhoof, Aran, Netherspite, and Nightbane" in one breath. That's ten names I've never seen with zero explanation of what any of them are.
- Step 30: switching to the Raids tab landed me scrolled to the bottom of the page with no heading in sight, like it forgot to scroll me back up.

#### What I liked
- The Ranked Gear tab (step 23): "#1 Furious Gizmatic Goggles" with a green "EQUIP FURIOUS GIZMATIC GOGGLES" button. One click and the button changed to a greyed-out "EQUIPPED HEAD" (step 24) — that's exactly the kind of clear yes/no feedback the other gear screen was missing.
- The Spec Tier List (step 28): S/A/B/C/D tiers is a format I already know from other games, and it tagged my own choice "Retribution — YOUR SPEC" right in the B row. Felt like it was actually talking to me.
- The loot list (step 34) has little orange honesty notes like "Mount, very low drop rate. Not in the item catalog" instead of pretending to know everything.
- The Professions page (step 35) is just a clean grid of icons and names — easiest screen on the whole site to scan.

#### What I never found, or gave up on
Nothing anywhere acknowledged that I might not be level 70 yet — no "here's what to do while leveling" path. I never worked out whether my 28.2 DPS number was good. I didn't get through the raid boss order/attunement paragraph, and I didn't open Raid Composition, Compare, Talents, Buffs, or Build — ran out of steam before getting there.

#### Words or labels I didn't understand
"Phase 2," "BiS" (it did spell out "Best in Slot (BiS)" once, which helped), "T4"/"T5," "attack-table," "white-damage," "glance" (as in miss/dodge/glance/crit), "Rotation coverage," "Stat Weights (EP)," "attunement," "Seal of Command"/"Judgement of Command"/"Seal of Blood," "Crusader Strike," and basically every raid boss name (Moroes, Attumen, Illhoof, Netherspite, Nightbane, etc.). Also "MP5" showed up on an item with no explanation.

#### Three things I would change
1. Notice that I'm not 70 and say so — even a small "you're leveling, here's what still applies" note would help.
2. Fix the gear-slot dropdown so picking an item always works the first time and actually shows on the character sheet — right now it silently fails sometimes.
3. Explain jargon where it first shows up (BiS, Phase 2, the DPS number's scale, boss names) instead of assuming I already raid.

#### Would I come back? Would I recommend it, and to whom?
Maybe, once I actually hit 70 — the Ranked Gear list and tier list are genuinely useful once I'm at that stage. I'd recommend it to a friend who already raids and knows the terms, not to another brand-new player like me.

#### Why I stopped
I'd gotten a real answer (the tier list placement and the BiS gear list), and everything past that — the simulation numbers, the raid attunement chain — needed game knowledge I don't have yet. Rather than keep clicking through paragraphs I couldn't parse, I called it.

#### Plan versus reality
My plan said I scan instead of read, click the shiniest thing first, and bounce if I feel lost for more than a few seconds. I did click straight into Character Planner and skipped every long paragraph exactly like I said I would. But I didn't bounce as fast as planned — the tier list tagging "YOUR SPEC" and the green Equip button were enough payoff to keep me going for the full session instead of leaving in the first few seconds.
