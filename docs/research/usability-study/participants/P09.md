## Their plan, written before the visit

### Plan — P09 (Gloria, 62)

#### What I usually do in my first minutes on a new site

- I read before I click anything. I like to see the whole first page and get my bearings before I touch the mouse.
- I look for a simple menu or a "start here" kind of link. If I don't see one quickly I get nervous I'm going to end up somewhere I didn't mean to go.
- I don't like clicking things unless I'm pretty sure what they'll do. If a button doesn't say plainly what happens, I'll hover over it first or just leave it alone.
- I zoom my browser in to 150% because small print is hard on my eyes, so I notice right away if text or buttons get too tiny or crowded once I do that.
- If I make a mistake I look for an obvious way to undo it or go back before I try anything else. I don't like to poke around and "see what happens" the way my grandson does.
- I'll stick with a site a little while if it feels safe and I can tell what's happening, but if I get confused or worried I might break something, I'd rather just stop and ask my grandson to show me instead.

#### What I'm hoping to get out of this visit

My grandson sent me a link to this and said it would help me figure out what my Night Elf Priest should wear — gear, I think he calls it. I don't really know these games well. I'm hoping I can find something simple that just tells me "wear this" or at least helps me understand if I'm doing it right, without needing him to walk me through it on the phone again.

## Their report, written after the visit

### P09: Gloria

#### In one paragraph
I came because my grandson sent me a link and said it would help me figure out what my Night Elf Priest should wear. It took some hunting and one guess I wasn't sure about, but I did end up finding a list that told me exactly what to put on, and I even found a page that told me my "Holy" choice was a good one, which was the reassurance I really wanted. It's clearly built by someone who knows this game very well and not necessarily for someone like me, but I got further than I expected to.

#### First impression
The first screen (step 1) was a big dramatic picture with "PROJECT DEFEAT" in large gold letters, "TBC Classic · Phase 2," and "For the Alliance and for the Horde," with five boxes below: Character Planner, Raid Composition, Spec Tier Lists, Raids, and Professions. My first thought was that this is a tool for people who already play this game seriously, not a beginner page — there was no "welcome" or "start here" telling me what to do first. I guessed that "Character Planner" was my way in because it mentioned "filling every slot," and I turned out to be right, but it was a guess, not something the page told me plainly.

#### What I came for, and what I actually did
I came to find out what my Priest should wear, and honestly whether I'm even playing her right. I went through the Character Planner's four steps — Faction, Race, Class, Specialization (steps 2–10) — picking Alliance, Night Elf, and Priest, which I knew, and then guessing "Holy" for Specialization since nothing explained the choices. That put me on a "Gear" screen full of empty boxes (step 11). I found a "Ranked Gear" tab (step 14) that gave me a plain, numbered list of what to wear per slot with an "Equip" button, and I used it to fill in my Head and Shoulders (steps 15, 27–28). I also checked "Spec Tier Lists" (steps 20–22) to see if Holy was a sensible pick, and saved my progress under the name "My Priest" before I left (steps 29–35).

#### Where I got stuck or confused
- **Step 8–10, the Specialization screen.** It asked me to choose Discipline, Holy, or Shadow with no explanation of what any of them meant. I hovered over "Holy" (step 9) hoping for a tooltip and nothing appeared. I only picked Holy because the word sounded like healing, which I vaguely remembered was my job. I got lucky — the pages after confirmed it — but I was genuinely guessing at something the site called a decision that "drives every ranking... from here on."
- **Step 7.** The sentence "Only the classes your race can actually be are offered" made me stop and read it twice. I think it's missing a word.
- **Steps 11–12.** Under "Key Totals" on the Gear screen there's a line that says "The rail carries all twenty-six." I have no idea what that means and it was never explained.
- **Step 14 onward.** Almost every recommended item had an orange note saying "Needs source/rank verification before treating as final." That made me second-guess whether I should trust the very list I'd just found.
- **Step 24.** I clicked the "RANKED GEAR" tab after scrolling down and got told "Nothing matching that is visible on the screen right now." I hadn't realized the row of tabs scrolls away and doesn't follow you down the page, so I had to scroll all the way back up.
- **Step 33.** I typed "My Priest" and pressed "Save," but the page still said "Nothing saved yet" afterward — nothing told me it had failed. I had to try clicking directly on the button again (step 35) before it actually took. If I hadn't happened to check, I'd have walked away thinking my work was safe when it wasn't.

#### What I liked
- The step-by-step wizard for Faction/Race/Class/Specialization (steps 2–8) was easy to follow, one question at a time, with a Back button if I changed my mind.
- The Ranked Gear list (step 14) was exactly what I wanted: a plain "#1" pick with a name and a big Equip button. When I used it, the button changed to say "Equipped Head" and the numbers in the middle of the screen visibly went up (steps 15–16), so I could tell it actually worked.
- The Healer tier list (step 21) listed "Holy — PRIEST — YOUR SPEC" right in the top "S" row. Seeing my own pick called out by name was the single most reassuring thing on the whole site.
- Being told plainly that "nothing is saved automatically" (step 19), so at least I knew to look for a save option before I left.

#### What I never found, or gave up on
I never found a way to fill in all the recommended gear at once — it's one slot, one click, twenty-six times (steps 17–18). I only did Head and Shoulders. I didn't get to Raid Composition, Raids, Professions, Compare, Talents, or Buffs at all; I ran out of steam after sorting out gear and my spec.

#### Words or labels I didn't understand
"TBC Classic," "Phase 2," "BiS" (I eventually worked out it means "Best in Slot" because the page spelled it out once), "DPS," "Tier 5 content," and the example text "Fury BiS, Prot offspec" in the save box (step 31) — none of those mean anything to me. I also never got a plain explanation of what "Discipline," "Holy," or "Shadow" actually do.

#### Three things I would change
1. Explain what Discipline, Holy, and Shadow mean in a short line right on that step (step 8) — the site clearly knows the difference later, since it calls my list "Priest Healing Best in Slot" once I'd already guessed right.
2. Give me a button to equip the whole recommended list at once, for someone who just wants the answer instead of 26 individual clicks.
3. Either finish or remove things like "Needs source/rank verification before treating as final" and "The rail carries all twenty-six" (steps 12, 14) — to a first-time visitor these read like unfinished notes, and they made me trust the recommendations less right when I needed to trust them most.

#### Would I come back? Would I recommend it, and to whom?
Maybe, but only with my grandson sitting next to me the first time to explain the words. I wouldn't hand this to a friend my age on her own without warning her it uses a lot of shorthand.

#### Why I stopped
I'd gotten real answers to both things I came for — a couple of pieces of gear picked out and confirmation that "Holy" was a sound choice — and the rest felt like repeating the same click twenty-four more times. My eyes were tired from all the small, dark text even zoomed in, so once I'd saved my progress (step 35) I decided that was a good place to leave it, the way I said I would in my plan.

#### Plan versus reality
In my plan I said I read before clicking, look for a "start here," avoid controls I don't understand, and would rather stop than poke around blindly. That mostly held: I hovered before choosing a specialization and double-checked the Gear tab after equipping something (step 16) instead of just trusting it. What I didn't expect was having to guess at a decision (Specialization) with zero help offered, or that a Save button would silently fail the first time (step 33) — that's exactly the kind of "did I just break something" moment I was worried about going in.
