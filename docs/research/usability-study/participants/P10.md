## Their report, written after the visit

### P10: Tyler

#### In one paragraph
Landed on the homepage, saw a card literally called "Spec Tier Lists," tapped it, and had my answer (Destruction Warlock, S tier) in two taps. That part was great — genuinely as fast as I wanted. Then I got curious about a "Simulation" tab, tapped "Run Simulation" without setting anything up, and got back "Estimated DPS: 35" talking about Bloodthirst and Whirlwind, which is warrior stuff — I don't even have a warrior. That's where I checked out.

#### First impression
Step 1: dark fantasy theme, "Project Defeat," "TBC Classic · Phase 2," "For the Alliance and for the Horde." Immediately read this as a WoW Classic (Burning Crusade) tool for raid planning — character gear, raid comps, tier lists. Correct guess, and it took about two seconds, which is all I give a site.

#### What I came for, and what I actually did
Came for: which class is best right now. Did: tapped the "Spec Tier Lists" card on the homepage (step 2), which dropped me straight into a DPS tier list — Arcane Mage, Destruction Warlock, Beast Mastery Hunter, and Fury Warrior all in S tier (step 2 screenshot). Scrolled down (step 3) and saw Affliction Warlock in A tier too. Got my answer inside of maybe 15 seconds of actual looking.

#### Where I got stuck or confused
- Step 5: tapped the "Destruction" row expecting it to expand or show more (it's styled like a card, looks tappable). Nothing happened at all.
- Steps 8-10: tapped the "Simulation" nav tab out of curiosity. It showed a dense paragraph of setup text, a "RUN SIMULATION" button, and a note saying "No run yet. Configure a character and start a simulation." I ignored the text and just mashed the button anyway. It ran and gave me "Estimated DPS: 35," with the explanation underneath mentioning Bloodthirst, Whirlwind, and Heroic Strike — those are warrior abilities. I never picked a character, let alone a warrior. The number meant nothing to me and actively confused me about whose DPS I was even looking at.

#### What I liked
The homepage card that says exactly what it does ("Spec Tier Lists — Wowhead's Phase 2 rankings for damage, healing and tanking — all three lists on one page") and then actually does that in one tap. Class name is right under the spec name on every row so I didn't have to know what "Destruction" means to know it's a Warlock. Tier groupings (S/A/B) are big and scannable one-handed.

#### What I never found, or gave up on
Never touched Character Planner, Raid Composition, Raids, or Professions — no reason to, once I had my tier list answer. Gave up on Simulation once it gave me a number tied to a class I never chose.

#### Words or labels I didn't understand
"Rotation coverage is the standing gap" and the rest of that Simulation intro paragraph — total wall of text, skipped it. "ROLE-AWARE PROTOTYPE" tag at the top of that section, no idea what that's telling me. "White-damage attack-table estimate" in the result text — not reading that on my phone.

#### Three things I would change
1. Don't let "Run Simulation" produce a confident-looking "Estimated DPS: 35" when no character is configured — it just looks like a real number for a real (wrong) character.
2. Make the tier list rows either actually do something when tapped, or make them look less like buttons.
3. Cut the Simulation intro paragraph down to a sentence. Nobody on a phone is reading five sentences before they even see a button.

#### Would I come back? Would I recommend it, and to whom?
Yeah, I'd come back to the tier list page specifically when a new phase drops — that part just works. I'd tell my guild "check the tier list page on this site, it's quick." I wouldn't point anyone to the Simulation tab off a phone.

#### Why I stopped
Got my actual answer fast (tier list), then poked at Simulation out of curiosity and got a result that felt broken/irrelevant to me. Not worth digging into on the couch with the TV on.

#### Plan versus reality
Matched my plan closely — scanned the first screen, tapped the first thing that matched what I wanted, didn't scroll more than needed, and bailed the moment something asked for setup/reading instead of just giving me the answer. The one thing I didn't expect: getting far enough to hit a confusing result at all, since I meant to leave right after the tier list.
