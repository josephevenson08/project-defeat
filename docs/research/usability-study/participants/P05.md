## Their plan, written before the visit

### Plan — P05, Ruth

#### What I usually do in the first few minutes on a new site
- I read the whole first screen before I tap anything — headlines, any little blurb, the menu bar — I don't like to poke around blind.
- I look for a menu or a list of sections first so I know what's even here before I go hunting for one specific thing.
- I'm careful with my tapping. On the iPad I've fat-fingered the wrong link plenty of times, so if buttons are small or crowded together I slow way down, sometimes zoom in with my fingers first.
- If I don't understand a word or a label, I'll pause on it rather than guess — I'd rather look silly asking than break something.
- I give a site a few minutes before I decide it's not for me. If I'm still lost after that, I probably close the tab and go back to trade chat or a forum where a person can just tell me the answer.
- I don't go looking for search boxes right away — I'd rather browse a menu than type, especially on the on-screen keyboard.

#### What I'm hoping to get out of this visit
Someone in trade chat mentioned this site for farming routes. I'm hoping to find where to farm herbs efficiently for my Alchemy, or some general tips on leveling the profession up. I'm not here for raiding or anything like that — just my Druid, her professions, and maybe some gathering routes I haven't thought of.

## Their report, written after the visit

### P05: Ruth

#### In one paragraph
I came in expecting a raiding site and mostly found one, but tucked inside it was a genuinely well-made Professions section that gave me exactly what I came for: a real herb farming route for my level with an actual map, and a full Alchemy recipe list that lined up with the herbs I'd be gathering. I hit one confusing moment with the back navigation and a couple of raid-jargon words I didn't know, but I got my answer quickly and didn't need to fight the site to get it.

#### First impression
The first screen (step 1) is just the title "Project Defeat," "TBC Classic · Phase 2," "For the Alliance and for the Horde," and five cards: Character Planner, Raid Composition, Spec Tier Lists, Raids, and Professions. My first thought was "this is a raider's site" — gear planning, raid rosters, loot tables, that's not my world. I scrolled to confirm that really was the whole front page (step 2) — it was, only 113 pixels of scroll, and no menu bar at all yet. I was right that it's raid-focused overall, but I was pleasantly surprised the Professions card turned out to be a real, separate thing for people like me, not just a footnote.

#### What I came for, and what I actually did
I wanted herb farming routes and help levelling Alchemy. I tapped Professions (step 3), got a full grid of every profession, and tapped Herbalism (step 4). I read through the levelling table and the "Where to farm" section, picked Durotar as my zone since I'm Horde (step 7), and found an actual route drawn on the zone map with stop counts (steps 8–9). I skipped ahead through the middle ranges (steps 10–11) to the top of the climb, skill 350–375, Netherstorm, which is my level (step 12). Then I went back to the profession grid (step 15, after a stumble — see below) and did the same for Alchemy (step 16), reading the "what it's worth at 70" info and the full crafting table down to 375 (steps 17–18). Out of curiosity I peeked at the Character Planner (step 19) and backed out once I saw it was a raid-build wizard, not for me (step 20).

#### Where I got stuck or confused
- **Step 13**: I was deep in the Herbalism page and tapped the "PROFESSIONS" tab in the top bar, expecting it to take me back to the list of professions the way a "back" button would. Nothing happened at all — I was still in the exact same spot (confirmed at step 14 when I scrolled up and found myself still on Herbalism). I only got back by noticing a small "← All professions" link near the top of the page (step 15), which I hadn't paid much attention to at first.
- **Steps 6–9**: I play a Tauren, so I looked for Mulgore among the starting-zone tabs for the earliest herbs and it wasn't one of the options (only Tirisfal Glades, Teldrassil, Elwynn Forest, Durotar, Azuremyst Isle, Dun Morogh, Silverpine Forest, and Loch Modan were tappable). It wasn't until I kept scrolling past the map, further than I normally would have, that I found a small line saying "Also recommended, no spawn coordinates in our ingest: Mulgore" (step 9). I would have missed that if I hadn't kept reading.
- **Steps 16–17**: On the Alchemy page there's an orange note about "Item ids 35750/35751 place these in the Sunwell tier" and "Individual stat blocks not read off tooltips." I didn't know what any of that meant and just scrolled past it.

#### What I liked
- The Herbalism and Alchemy pages themselves: clean tables of skill range, what to gather or craft, and where, plus real zone maps with dots and lines showing an actual loop — "119 recorded spawns · 20 stops" for Durotar (step 8), "189 recorded spawns · 32 stops" for Netherstorm at my level (step 12). That's exactly the kind of thing I was hoping trade chat was talking about.
- The small "Worth knowing" callout boxes that explain things in plain language, like the one saying gathering professions don't give a combat bonus in this expansion.
- The honesty about the data — it says a route is "a strong starting line rather than a proven optimum" and admits outright when it doesn't have real spawn data for a zone instead of pretending it does.
- The Alchemy recipe table used the same herb names as the Herbalism page, so the two sides of my plan actually talked to each other.

#### What I never found, or gave up on
I never found a proper mapped route for my own starting zone, Mulgore — just the one-line mention. I didn't go into Raid Composition, Spec Tier Lists, or Raids at all, and backed out of the Character Planner (step 20) as soon as I saw it was a four-step Faction/Race/Class/Specialization wizard for raid gear — not what I came for. I also didn't click the external "Levelling guide on wow-professions.com" or "Specializations" links on the Alchemy page; I'd rather stay on a site I'm still getting to know than click off to somewhere new.

#### Words or labels I didn't understand
"Sunwell tier" and the item id numbers on the Alchemy page (step 16); "individual stat blocks not read off tooltips" right next to it. I worked out "Transmute Master" and the elixir/potion specialisation choice from context, but it took a moment.

#### Three things I would change
1. Make the "PROFESSIONS" tab in the top bar actually return you to the profession list (or at least scroll to top) even when you're already inside one profession's page — right now tapping it while deep in Herbalism does nothing, which had me thinking I'd broken something.
2. Give Mulgore (and I'd guess Eversong Woods) the same proper map treatment as the other starting zones, or at least say why they're missing right in the tab row instead of only in a line of text much further down.
3. Either drop the raid-gearing jargon on the Alchemy page (Sunwell tier, item ids, stat blocks) or explain it in one of those "Worth knowing" boxes like the rest of the page does.

#### Would I come back? Would I recommend it, and to whom?
Yes, for the Professions section specifically — it did exactly what I needed. I'd recommend it to a guildmate working on their gathering professions. I wouldn't personally use the raid-planning side, but I wouldn't tell anyone to avoid it either — it's just not built for me.

#### Why I stopped
I found both things I came for — a real farming route for my herb level and a full Alchemy recipe list — and confirmed the rest of the site is for raiders, which I'm not. Once I had my answer there wasn't a reason to keep going.

#### Plan versus reality
My plan said I'd read the whole screen before tapping, look for a menu before hunting for one thing, and go slow on the iPad since small buttons trip me up. That's how it went — I read the whole front page before tapping anything, and the one moment that actually tripped me up wasn't a tiny button, it was the top nav tab doing nothing when I expected it to act like "back." I said I'd rather browse a menu than search, which held up — I never saw or looked for a search box. I also said I'd give a site a few minutes before writing it off; the Professions section won me over well inside that window.
