Tier 5: Destroyer Battlegear

Source: Wowhead · Phase 2 · updated 12 Sep (3 wk ago)

Four Destroyer pieces are in this set. Each comes from a T5 token, so the boss that drops the token is the one to farm.

  • Destroyer Battle-HelmLady Vashj — Serpentshrine Cavern
  • Destroyer ShoulderbladesVoid Reaver — Tempest Keep
  • Destroyer BreastplateKael'thas Sunstrider — Tempest Keep
  • Destroyer GauntletsLeotheras the Blind — Serpentshrine Cavern
  • Fifth piece (legs) not wornLeggings of Murderous Intent win the slot

Wearing four of five pieces keeps the 4-piece bonus while letting a stronger non-set item take the legs.

Both trinkets and the neck are Phase 1 drops that still hold up. Kael'thas Sunstrider drops three of the pieces here, which makes him the boss to plan the week around.

Enchants at a glance

Eleven of the seventeen slots carry an enchant: Mongoose on both weapons, Glyph of Ferocity on the head, Might of the Scourge on the shoulders, Nethercobra Leg Armor on the legs and Khorium Scope on the Serpent Spine Longbow, plus the cloak, chest, wrist, hand and boot enchants.

Enchants are listed under each item in the table above, so this section only tells you which ones need a trip somewhere.
