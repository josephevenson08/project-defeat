Raid Composition · Groups of five

Seat your raid so every group gets its buffs.

In TBC, 24 of the 33 raid buffs reach only the caster's own party: every totem, every aura, both shouts. Click specs to fill the seats, move people between groups, and watch what each group receives.

Raid table Empty raid · click a spec below, or load the example

Counts are who could cast it, not what will be up at once. This follows Wowhead's raid-composition tool: one Paladin lights up every Greater Blessing here but holds one in the game; the same goes for a Shaman's totems and a Warrior's shouts.

Add a player · next seat: group 1, seat 1

Click a spec to seat it in the next open seat, filling group 1 first. Press a group's name to fill that group instead. To move someone, press Move on their seat, then the seat you want (an occupied seat swaps), or drag them there. The pencil names a player. Esc cancels.

Role balance 0 of 25 seated

Role comes from the build a seat signed up as, so a Feral (Bear) counts as a tank and a Feral (Cat) as physical DPS. Both bring Leader of the Pack. Source: raidBuilds.ts, tbcClasses.ts.
