Raid Composition · Terrace of Light

Seat your raid in the light, five to a ring.

In TBC, 24 of the 33 raid buffs reach only the caster's own party: every totem, every aura, both shouts. Fill the seats below, move people between groups, and watch what each group receives.

Raid table Empty raid · start by adding a player or load the example

Counts are who could cast it, not what will be up at once. This follows Wowhead's raid-composition tool: one Paladin lights up every Greater Blessing here but holds one in the game; the same goes for a Shaman's totems and a Warrior's shouts.
