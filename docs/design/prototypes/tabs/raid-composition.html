Raid Composition · groups of five · Serpentshrine light

Seat your raid so every group gets its buffs.

In TBC, 24 of the 33 raid buffs reach only the caster's own party: every totem, every aura, both shouts. Where a player sits decides who gets them. Seat a 10 or 25-player raid, see what each group receives, and what one more seat would add.

Groups example roster · real specs

Counts are who could cast it, not what will be up at once. This follows Wowhead's raid-composition tool: one Paladin lights up every Greater Blessing here but holds one in the game; the same goes for a Shaman's totems and a Warrior's shouts.

To move a player, press Move on their seat, then Here on the seat you want. The two swap. Esc cancels.

Raid-wide, single-target and on the boss

Greater Blessings reach the whole raid, single-target buffs go wherever the caster points them, and debuffs land on the boss. Seating does not change these, only who is in the raid.
