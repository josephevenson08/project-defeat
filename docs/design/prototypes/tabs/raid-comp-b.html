Raid Composition · the muster board · Hellfire war camp

Muster your raid so every banner gets its buffs.

24 of the 33 raid buffs in TBC reach only the caster's own party: every totem, every aura, both shouts. Seat a 10 or 25-player raid on the board and see, group by group, what each banner receives and what it still lacks.

Muster board 0 of 25 seated

Counts are who could cast it, not what will be up at once. This follows Wowhead's raid-composition tool: one Paladin lights up every Greater Blessing here but holds one in the game; the same goes for a Shaman's totems and a Warrior's shouts.

Press Add on an open seat to pick a class and spec. To move a player, press Move, then Here on the seat you want; if it is taken, the two swap. Esc cancels. With a mouse you can also drag a player onto another seat.

The board is empty. Seat players one by one, or load the example roster.

Role balance 0 of 25 seated

Role comes from the build a seat signed up as, so a Feral (Bear) counts as a tank and a Feral (Cat) as physical DPS. Both bring Leader of the Pack. Source: raidBuilds.ts, tbcClasses.ts.

What one more seat would add

For each banner, the one build that would bring that group the most party buffs it lacks.
