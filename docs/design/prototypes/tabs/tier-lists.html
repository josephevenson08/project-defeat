Reading the lists tierLists.json · TierListsPanel.tsx

Specs, not items

A placement says how a spec performs in Tier 5 content. It does not rank gear, and the planner's per-slot gear rankings are not built from it.

Wowhead's words stay on Wowhead

Only tier letters and spec placements are stored. The per-spec analysis and tier definitions are Wowhead's authored prose and are deliberately not copied; each list links to its source page.

Across the lists

Feral Druid is the one spec on two lists: C for damage and S for tanking. Warriors appear twice too: Fury at S for damage, Protection at B for tanking.

Tier letters are drawn neutral rather than in Wowhead's quality colours, because quality colour means "this item is epic" everywhere else in the app. Rank reads through letter weight and rule strength; the coloured edge on each spec is its Blizzard class colour.
