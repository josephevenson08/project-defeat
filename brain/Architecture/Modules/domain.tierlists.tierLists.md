> Wowhead's Phase 2 spec tier lists, generated by `tools/ingest/ingest-tier-lists.mjs`.
> 
> Three lists, 28 placements, covering all 27 specs the app knows — every spec appears on at least
> one list, and Feral Druid appears on two.
> 
> **These rank specs, not items.** They cannot narrow a per-slot BiS list and are not wired into one;
> the two datasets answer different questions and the tier list is the weaker of the two for gearing.
> 
> The tier *letters and membership* are here. Wowhead's analysis prose is not, deliberately — that is
> their writing rather than data, so each list carries `sourceUrl` and the view links out to it.
