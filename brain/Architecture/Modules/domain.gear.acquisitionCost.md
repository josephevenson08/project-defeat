From the top of the file:

> What a crafted or bought BiS pick costs, generated by `tools/ingest/ingest-acquisition-cost.mts`.
> 
> **"Where" was only half a plan.** `resolveAcquisition` will tell you a robe is Tailoring and a
> trinket is bought from G'eras, and both then read as "go and get it" — the robe wants 14 Primal
> Fire, and the trinket wants 41 Badges of Justice, which is a fortnight of heroics. Those two
> facts belong on the same row as the recommendation, not in a browser tab beside it.
> 
> Read from Wowhead's own item pages: reagents come from the `created-by-spell` listview, prices from
> `sold-by`. Nothing is estimated, so there is no `needsVerification` here — an item this has no
> entry for is simply absent, which the surface reports as "no cost recorded" rather than as free.
