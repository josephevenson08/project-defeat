# domain.consumables.sampleConsumables

`src/domain/consumables/sampleConsumables.ts` · **domain** layer · 31 lines

From the top of the file:

> Raid consumables, generated by `tools/ingest/ingest-consumables.mjs`.
> 
> Two sources: the stat values come from wowsims (the numbers its sim runs on), and the names and
> item ids from Wowhead, because wowsims only carries protobuf enum names. That split matters —
> transforming `AdeptsElixir` into a display name loses the apostrophe, and the lookup is what turns
> it back into "Adept's Elixir". It also caught `ElixirOfMajorFirePower`, which is really
> "Elixir of Major Firepower", one word.
> 
> Roles are derived from the stats each consumable grants rather than assigned per spec. The Wowhead
> consumables guides do publish per-spec advice, but in prose, and inventing a per-spec opinion from
> it is exactly the kind of plausible-looking guesswork that made the old item catalogue untrustworthy.
> A flask granting attack power serves physical damage; that much the data actually says.
