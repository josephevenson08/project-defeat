# Tab Prototypes

_Seven pages, one per tab of the site, each with its own scene colour over the same crystal and water._

Each page shares one scene, a naaru-like crystal over water, and gives it its own colour and one small detail (decided 2026-10-05, built 2026-10-07). The Dark Portal designs for Home were dropped that day. Since 2026-10-10 each page's links, headings and buttons take its own colour too.

| Tab | Page | Scene | Accent | Where it stands |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | `home.html` | Teal, the original; one soft crystal swell per session | Teal | Settled |
| Character Planner | `planner.html` | Deep sea blue, rising bubbles | Sky blue | All six sub-tabs decided |
| Simulation | `simulation.html` | Gold over violet, crystal pillars | Gold | Decided; runs the live app's real simulator |
| Raid Composition | `raid-composition.html` | Warm white-gold, one light per raid group | Gold | Decided |
| Spec Tier Lists | `tier-lists.html` | Emerald, rank rings under the crystal | Emerald | Decided; Wowhead's tiers beside the logs |
| Raids | `raids.html` | Follows the chosen raid | Follows the raid | Decided; reads the live app's raid data |
| Professions | `professions.html` | Amber, ore veins up the pillars | Amber | Waits for a later update |

Moving between tabs is a plain link. A full-screen curtain was tried and removed at the owner's request (2026-10-08).
