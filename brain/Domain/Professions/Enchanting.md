# Enchanting

**Crafting** profession



## Skill tiers

- **Apprentice** — skill 1–75, level 5+ · Any profession trainer for this skill in a major Azeroth city (Stormwind, Ironforge, Darnassus, Exodar / Orgrimmar, Undercity, Thunder Bluff, Silvermoon).
- **Journeyman** — skill 75–150, level 10+ · Any profession trainer for this skill in a major Azeroth city (Stormwind, Ironforge, Darnassus, Exodar / Orgrimmar, Undercity, Thunder Bluff, Silvermoon).
- **Expert** — skill 150–225, level 20+ · Any profession trainer for this skill in a major Azeroth city (Stormwind, Ironforge, Darnassus, Exodar / Orgrimmar, Undercity, Thunder Bluff, Silvermoon).
- **Artisan** — skill 225–300, level 35+ · Any profession trainer for this skill in a major Azeroth city (Stormwind, Ironforge, Darnassus, Exodar / Orgrimmar, Undercity, Thunder Bluff, Silvermoon).
- **Master** — skill 300–375, level 50+ · Master Enchanting trainer in Outland: Johan Barnes (Alliance, Honor Hold) / Felannia (Horde, Thrallmar); neutral options Asarnan (Consortium, Stormspire, Netherstorm) or High Enchanter Bardolan (Scryers, Shattrath, requires Scryers reputation).



## Leveling path

- skill 1–300: **Standard vanilla enchant progression (weapon/armor enchants using Strange/Soul/Vision Dust etc.)** — Trainer-taught, supplemented by disenchanting drops for materials.
- skill 300–310: **1x Runed Fel Iron Rod, then 9x Bracer - Assault** — Master Enchanting trainer (Outland). · 1 Fel Iron Rod, 4 Greater Eternal Essence, 6 Large Brilliant Shard for the rod, 54 Arcane Dust
- skill 310–316: **6x Bracer - Brawn** — Master Enchanting trainer (Outland). · 36 Arcane Dust
- skill 316–330: **16x Gloves - Assault** — Master Enchanting trainer (Outland). · 128 Arcane Dust
- skill 330–335: **5x Shield - Major Stamina** — Recipe sold by Madame Ruby in Shattrath City. · 75 Arcane Dust
- skill 335–340: **5x Shield - Resilience** — Master Enchanting trainer (Outland). · 5 Large Prismatic Shard, 20 Lesser Planar Essence
- skill 340–350: **15x Superior Wizard Oil** — Recipe sold by Madame Ruby in Shattrath City. · 45 Arcane Dust, 15 Nightmare Vine, 15 Imbued Vial
- skill 350–360: **15x Enchant Gloves - Major Strength** — Master Enchanting trainer (Outland). · 180 Arcane Dust, 15 Greater Planar Essence
- skill 360–361: **1x Runed Adamantite Rod** — Formula sold by Rungor (Stonebreaker Hold, Terokkar Forest) or Vodesiin (Temple of Telhamat, Hellfire Peninsula). · 1 Adamantite Rod, 1 Primal Might, 8 Greater Planar Essence, 8 Large Prismatic Shard
- skill 361–365: **10x Enchant Gloves - Major Strength** — Master Enchanting trainer (Outland). · 120 Arcane Dust, 10 Greater Planar Essence
- skill 365–375: **12x Enchant Ring - Spellpower** — Formula sold by Alurmi (Tanaris); needs Honored with Keepers of Time. · 24 Large Prismatic Shard, 24 Greater Planar Essence
