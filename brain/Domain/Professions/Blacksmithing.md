# Blacksmithing

**Crafting** profession



## Skill tiers

- **Apprentice** — skill 1–75, level 5+ · Any profession trainer for this skill in a major Azeroth city (Stormwind, Ironforge, Darnassus, Exodar / Orgrimmar, Undercity, Thunder Bluff, Silvermoon).
- **Journeyman** — skill 75–150, level 10+ · Any profession trainer for this skill in a major Azeroth city (Stormwind, Ironforge, Darnassus, Exodar / Orgrimmar, Undercity, Thunder Bluff, Silvermoon).
- **Expert** — skill 150–225, level 20+ · Any profession trainer for this skill in a major Azeroth city (Stormwind, Ironforge, Darnassus, Exodar / Orgrimmar, Undercity, Thunder Bluff, Silvermoon).
- **Artisan** — skill 225–300, level 35+ · Any profession trainer for this skill in a major Azeroth city (Stormwind, Ironforge, Darnassus, Exodar / Orgrimmar, Undercity, Thunder Bluff, Silvermoon).
- **Master** — skill 300–375, level 50+ · Master Blacksmithing trainer in Outland: Humphry (Alliance, Honor Hold) / Rohok (Horde, Thrallmar), Hellfire Peninsula.



## Leveling path

- skill 1–300: **Standard vanilla weapon/armor progression through Thorium-tier gear** — Trainer-taught, with a few BoE plans from vendors/AH.
- skill 300–305: **7x Fel Weightstone** — Master Blacksmithing trainer (Outland). · 7 Fel Iron Bar, 7 Netherweave Cloth
- skill 305–316: **11x Fel Iron Plate Belt** — Master Blacksmithing trainer (Outland). · 44 Fel Iron Bar
- skill 316–321: **5x Fel Iron Chain Gloves** — Master Blacksmithing trainer (Outland). · 25 Fel Iron Bar
- skill 321–325: **4x Fel Iron Plate Boots** — Master Blacksmithing trainer (Outland). · 24 Fel Iron Bar
- skill 325–335: **45x Lesser Rune of Warding** — Master Blacksmithing trainer (Outland). · 45 Adamantite Bar
- skill 335–340: **7x Fel Iron Chain Tunic** — Master Blacksmithing trainer (Outland). · 63 Fel Iron Bar
- skill 340–350: **45x Lesser Ward of Shielding** — Recipe sold by Rohok (Horde, Hellfire Peninsula) or Mari Stonehand (Alliance, Shadowmoon Valley). · 45 Adamantite Bar
- skill 350–360: **45x Adamantite Weightstone** — Recipe sold by Fedryen Swiftspear (Zangarmarsh); needs Honored with Cenarion Expedition. · 45 Adamantite Bar, 90 Netherweave Cloth
- skill 360–375: **17x Enchanted Adamantite Belt, or Felsteel Gloves** — Enchanted Adamantite Belt from Quartermaster Enuril at Friendly with The Scryers; Plans: Felsteel Gloves drops from Auchenai Monks in Auchenai Crypts, and only with a Blacksmith in the group. · 34 Hardened Adamantite Bar (~680 Adamantite Ore), 136 Arcane Dust, 34 Large Prismatic Shard
- skill 200–200: **Specialization choice: Armorsmith or Weaponsmith (Swordsmith / Hammersmith / Axesmith)** — Quest-unlocked specialization trainers; each grants access to exclusive high-end plate/weapon plans with phase-gated upgrades.
