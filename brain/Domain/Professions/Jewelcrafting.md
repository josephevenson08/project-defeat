# Jewelcrafting

**Crafting** profession



## Skill tiers

- **Apprentice** — skill 1–75, level 5+ · Any profession trainer for this skill in a major Azeroth city (Stormwind, Ironforge, Darnassus, Exodar / Orgrimmar, Undercity, Thunder Bluff, Silvermoon).
- **Journeyman** — skill 75–150, level 10+ · Any profession trainer for this skill in a major Azeroth city (Stormwind, Ironforge, Darnassus, Exodar / Orgrimmar, Undercity, Thunder Bluff, Silvermoon).
- **Expert** — skill 150–225, level 20+ · Any profession trainer for this skill in a major Azeroth city (Stormwind, Ironforge, Darnassus, Exodar / Orgrimmar, Undercity, Thunder Bluff, Silvermoon).
- **Artisan** — skill 225–300, level 35+ · Any profession trainer for this skill in a major Azeroth city (Stormwind, Ironforge, Darnassus, Exodar / Orgrimmar, Undercity, Thunder Bluff, Silvermoon).
- **Master** — skill 300–375, level 50+ · Master Jewelcrafting trainer in Outland: Tatiana (Alliance, Honor Hold) / Kalaen (Horde, Thrallmar), Hellfire Peninsula, or neutral Xyrol at Area 52, Netherstorm.



## Leveling path

- skill 1–20: **Delicate Copper Wire (component crafting)** — Trainer-taught starting recipe.
- skill 20–30: **Rough Stone Statue** — Trainer-taught.
- skill 30–50: **Tigerseye Band** — Trainer-taught.
- skill 130–200: **Pendant of the Agate Shield and similar bronze-setting designs** — Design purchased from vendors in Thousand Needles or Wetlands. · Bronze Setting, Moss Agate
- skill 200–300: **Mithril/Truesilver/Thorium-set rings, culminating in Emerald Lion Ring** — Mostly trainer-taught, with a few vendor-bought designs.
- skill 300–320: **~30 uncommon gem cuts, mixed across Brilliant Golden Draenite, Glowing Shadow Draenite, Inscribed Flame Spessarite, Radiant Deep Peridot, Solid Azure Moonstone and Teardrop Blood Garnet** — Designs sold by Kalaen / Tatiana in Hellfire Peninsula. · One raw gem per cut — prospect Fel Iron Ore for the lot
- skill 320–325: **5-7x of any one of Glinting Flame Spessarite, Bright Blood Garnet, Jagged Deep Peridot or Sparkling Azure Moonstone** — Designs sold by Kalaen / Tatiana in Hellfire Peninsula.
- skill 325–335: **12x Mercurial Adamantite** — Master Jewelcrafting trainer (Outland). · 48 Adamantite Powder — roughly 240 Adamantite Ore prospected, 12 Primal Earth
- skill 335–340: **5-10x of any one of Potent Flame Spessarite, Sovereign Shadow Draenite or Smooth Golden Draenite** — Sovereign and Smooth from Kalaen / Tatiana; Potent Flame Spessarite from Nakodu, which needs Friendly with Lower City.
- skill 340–350: **12x Heavy Adamantite Ring** — Master Jewelcrafting trainer (Outland). · 12 Adamantite Bar, 12 Mercurial Adamantite — the ones saved at 325
- skill 350–360: **15x Purified Shadow Pearl** — Master Jewelcrafting trainer (Outland). · 15 Shadow Pearl, 15 Purified Draenic Water — any Outland innkeeper
- skill 360–365: **5x of a Jewelcrafting-only epic gem — Crimson Sun, Don Julio's Heart, Falling Star, Blood of Amber, Stone of Blades, Facet of Eternity or Kailee's Rose** — All reputation-gated: Consortium Revered (Crimson Sun, Don Julio's Heart), Lower City Revered (Falling Star), Sha'tar Honored/Revered (Kailee's Rose, Blood of Amber), Keepers of Time Honored/Revered (Facet of Eternity, Stone of Blades).
- skill 365–375: **10x meta gem cuts** — Designs are reputation-gated — Insightful Earthstorm Diamond from Almaador at Sha'tar reputation, among others. · 10 Earthstorm Diamond or 10 Skyfire Diamond
