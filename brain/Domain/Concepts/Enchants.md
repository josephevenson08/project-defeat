# Enchants

_Permanent slot upgrades, with real Phase 1/2 availability gaps._

Enchants are modelled per slot with role targeting. The gaps matter as much as the entries: casters and healers had no cloak or leg-armour enchant this early in TBC, so their absence in the data is intentional rather than missing coverage.

Weapon enchants are the largest single upgrade in the system — Mongoose for physical DPS, Soulfrost/Sunfire for casters — and several drop as formulas from raid bosses rather than being trainable.
