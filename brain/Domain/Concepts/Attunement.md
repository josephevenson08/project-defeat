# Attunement

_The quest chains that gate raid entry, and why they shape the raid week._

TBC gates its raids behind attunement chains rather than item level. The chains run through heroic dungeons and earlier raids, which is why Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, and Magtheridon's Lair stay in the weekly rotation long after their loot stops mattering — they hold the keys to Serpentshrine Cavern and Tempest Keep.

Three of the five raids have their full ordered chain step by step (Karazhan, Serpentshrine Cavern and Tempest Keep), shown on each raid's "About the raid" page in the Raids tab; Gruul's Lair and Magtheridon's Lair need none, and their page says so.
