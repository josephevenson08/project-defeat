# Lady Vashj

[[Serpentshrine Cavern]] · encounter 6

## Mechanics

Three phases and the hardest fight in Serpentshrine Cavern. Phase 1 is a spread-out tank fight — Static Charge chains to anyone nearby and Shock Blast one-shots whoever it lands on. Phase 2 makes her immune and puts the raid on four shield generators: Tainted Elementals drop Tainted Cores that must be hand-passed to the generators, while Coilfang Striders are ranged-tanked and Enchanted Elementals are stopped before they reach her. Phase 3 returns to Phase 1 mechanics plus endlessly spawning Toxic Spore Bats that leave poison clouds, making it a hard soft-enrage.

## Per-role

- [[Tank]] — Threat fully resets entering Phase 3 — do not let a DPS open before the tank has re-established.
- [[Physical DPS]] — Phase 2 is entirely about the Tainted Core relay; assign core runners in advance and do not improvise.
- [[Healer]] — Phase 3 spore bat poison stacks on everyone; dispel priority is the tank, then core runners.
