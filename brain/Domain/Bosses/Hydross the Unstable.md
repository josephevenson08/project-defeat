# Hydross the Unstable

[[Serpentshrine Cavern]] · encounter 1

## Mechanics

A two-tank resistance fight. Hydross flips between a frost form and a nature form each time he is dragged across the line in the middle of his platform, and each flip spawns four elemental adds of the opposite type. Both forms stack a Mark debuff on everyone every 15 seconds that only resets on a form change, which is the real timer. The frost tank needs a frost resistance set and the nature tank needs a nature resistance set; running this fight without resistance gear is how it fails.

## Per-role

- [[Tank]] — Two tanks, one in a frost resistance set and one in a nature resistance set — they swap on every form change.
- [[Physical DPS]] — Spread for Water Tomb in frost phase; the debuff stuns and damages anyone nearby.
- [[Healer]] — Vile Sludge in nature phase halves the target's output and must be cleansed off healers first.
