# Al'ar

[[Tempest Keep - The Eye]] · encounter 1

## Mechanics

Phase 1 has Al'ar cycling clockwise between four platforms every 30 seconds, dropping a Phoenix Hawk add on each move; a tank per platform picks the boss up as it lands and melee kill the adds. Flame Quills fires from the centre and forces the whole raid to run to the outer ring. Phase 2 begins at 0% health in Phase 1 — the boss lands in the middle, applies a stacking Melt Armor to the tank that forces swaps, Dive Bombs a random spot leaving a fire patch and an Ember of Al'ar, and Charges random players. Ranged damage carries this fight; melee spend most of Phase 1 on adds.

## Per-role

- [[Tank]] — Four platform tanks in Phase 1, then a two-tank Melt Armor swap in Phase 2.
- [[Physical DPS]] — Melee handle the Phoenix Hawks in Phase 1; someone must stay in melee range or Flame Buffet hits the raid.
