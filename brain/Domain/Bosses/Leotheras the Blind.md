# Leotheras the Blind

[[Serpentshrine Cavern]] · encounter 3

## Mechanics

Alternates every 45 seconds between a humanoid form that Whirlwinds and wipes threat afterwards, and a demon form that must be tanked by a warlock in fire resistance gear because of Chaos Blast. In demon form he casts Insidious Whisper on five players, spawning an Inner Demon copy that each of those players has to kill alone within 30 seconds or be permanently mind-controlled. Everyone stops attacking through the Whirlwind and lets the tank rebuild threat afterwards. At 15% both forms split apart and the fight becomes a straight burn.

## Per-role

- [[Tank]] — A warlock tanks the demon form in fire resistance gear; the main tank only handles the humanoid form.
- [[Caster DPS]] — Kill your own Inner Demon fast — nobody else can help, and a survivor mind-controls you for the rest of the fight.
- [[Physical DPS]] — Stop damage entirely during Whirlwind; the threat wipe afterwards kills whoever pushed through it.
