# Fathom-Lord Karathress

[[Serpentshrine Cavern]] · encounter 4

## Mechanics

Four separate targets, each on its own tank. Karathress absorbs the abilities of every advisor that dies before him, so the kill order matters more than raw damage: Fathom-Guard Tidalvess first (Spitfire Totem must be killed on sight), then Sharkkis (his pet needs its own tank), then Caribdis (interrupt her heals, and she is pulled to a far corner so her Water Bolt Volley cannot reach the raid). Karathress himself is held above 75% until the advisors are dead, or Blessing of the Tides makes him unkillable.

## Per-role

- [[Tank]] — Four tanks minimum, plus one for Sharkkis's pet. Assign every target by name before the pull.
- [[Caster DPS]] — Caribdis is a caster-group kill and her heals must be interrupted on a rotation.
- [[Physical DPS]] — Melee sit on Karathress but swap instantly to every Spitfire Totem that Tidalvess drops.
