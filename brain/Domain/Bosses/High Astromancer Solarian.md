# High Astromancer Solarian

[[Tempest Keep - The Eye]] · encounter 3

## Mechanics

Three phases on a fixed timer. In Phase 1 she has no threat table and fires Arcane Missiles at random players, and Wrath of the Astromancer puts a bomb on someone who must run at least 20 yards clear before it detonates and knocks the raid around. Phase 2 spawns Solarium Agents from three portals plus Solarium Priests, which are stunned and interrupted and die first. At 20% she becomes a large Void Walker with Void Bolt and Psychic Scream — Fear Ward on the tank and a Tremor Totem down make the last phase manageable.

## Per-role

- [[Tank]] — Fear in the final phase will drag you into the room; keep Fear Ward or a Tremor Totem covering you.
- [[Caster DPS]] — Wrath of the Astromancer means run out, immediately, every time — the knockback wipes clumped raids.
