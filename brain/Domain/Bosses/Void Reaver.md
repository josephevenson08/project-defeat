# Void Reaver

[[Tempest Keep - The Eye]] · encounter 2

## Mechanics

Mechanically the simplest boss in Tier 5 and famous for gearing raids fast. Knock Away throws the tank back and wipes their threat entirely, so tanks rotate and everyone else keeps threat well under the cap. Pounding is a channelled AoE that hits everything in melee range and is the main healing load. Arcane Orb launches a slow-moving orb at a random ranged player that silences and damages on contact — ranged spread out and simply walk around it.

## Per-role

- [[Tank]] — Threat is fully wiped by Knock Away; plan a tank rotation rather than trying to out-threat it.
- [[Caster DPS]] — Stay at least 18 yards out and side-step Arcane Orbs; a silence at the wrong moment is the usual cause of deaths.
- [[Healer]] — Pounding is predictable raid damage on melee — pre-cast into it rather than reacting.
