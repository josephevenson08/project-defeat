# The Lurker Below

[[Serpentshrine Cavern]] · encounter 2

## Mechanics

Summoned by fishing the strange pool in the middle of the platform ring, which needs 300 fishing skill — clear the surrounding platforms first or the pull is unsurvivable. Spout is a rotating water beam that knocks everyone it touches into the water; the standard answer is for the whole raid to jump into the water before it starts and climb back out after. He casts Water Bolt on the raid if nobody is in melee range, so melee never all leave at once. Every two minutes he submerges and spawns Coilfang Ambushers and Guardians that have to be crowd-controlled or focused down.

## Per-role

- [[Tank]] — Three tanks: one on the boss, two on the add packs during each submerge phase.
- [[Physical DPS]] — Melee must keep someone in range at all times or Water Bolt goes out on the raid.
