# Kael'thas Sunstrider

[[Tempest Keep - The Eye]] · encounter 4

## Mechanics

A five-phase, 20-plus-minute encounter and the hardest fight of Phase 2. Phase 1 is the four advisors one at a time: Thaladred fixates a random player, Lord Sanguinar fears, Capernian is tanked at range by a warlock, and Telonicus applies a ranged-attack-speed debuff. Phase 2 spawns the seven Sunstrider weapons, which must be destroyed inside two minutes; players loot the Infinity Blade and Cosmic Infuser to use in Phase 3, where all four advisors return at once. Phase 4 is Kael'thas himself with Phoenixes, Flamestrike, and Mind Control, and Phase 5 at 50% adds Gravity Lapse, which throws the raid into the air and turns the fight into a movement puzzle.

## Per-role

- [[Tank]] — One tank kites the Phoenix in Phase 4; the advisor tanks must be pre-assigned by name for both Phase 1 and Phase 3.
- [[Physical DPS]] — Loot the Infinity Blade in Phase 2 — its on-hit dispel is what breaks the Phase 4 Mind Controls.
- [[Caster DPS]] — A warlock tanks Capernian at range; everyone else stays spread through Gravity Lapse.
- [[Healer]] — Gravity Lapse removes your ability to stand still and cast — plan instant heals and pre-hots for it.
