# Morogrim Tidewalker

[[Serpentshrine Cavern]] · encounter 5

## Mechanics

Earthquake damages everyone within 50 yards and spawns two packs of six murlocs from the room entrances; those packs are kited into the boss and AoE'd down, which is where a paladin off-tank earns their place. Watery Grave teleports random players to the waterfall behind the boss, stuns them, and drops them for fall damage — those players need to be healed back up on arrival. Tidal Wave is a frontal cone, so the boss faces away from the raid. Below 25% the murlocs are replaced by Water Globules that fixate on players and explode on contact, turning the end into a kiting damage race.

## Per-role

- [[Tank]] — Two extra tanks hold the murloc packs at the doorways; paladins handle this best because of consecration threat.
- [[Healer]] — Players in Watery Grave are teleported away and take fall damage — watch the raid frame, not the room.
