## Their plan, written before the visit

### Plan: P01 (Dana)

#### What I usually do on a new site
- I give it about 15-30 seconds to tell me what it is before I decide if it's worth my time. If the homepage is a wall of text or a login wall, I'm out.
- I don't read, I scan. Headers, big buttons, anything bolded. If I have to read a paragraph to know what to click, that's a bad sign.
- I look for my own situation first — in this case, "Hunter" and "Phase 2" — before I care about anything else on the site.
- I don't make an account for a tool I'm just trying out. If it demands a login before showing me anything useful, I'll probably bail.
- If a guildmate vouched for it ("neat for gear"), I'll push through one or two confusing screens I wouldn't otherwise bother with. But not more than that — I've got maybe 20 minutes after the kids are down.
- I'm not going to hunt through menus. If what I want isn't obvious in two or three clicks, I'll assume it's not there.

#### What I'm hoping to get out of this visit
Some guildmate dropped this link in Discord saying it's "neat for gear." I played Hunter back in the day and I'm back for the Anniversary realm. I honestly don't remember item names anymore, just raid names (Karazhan, SSC, that kind of thing). I want to know, in plain terms, what I should be farming or rolling on right now for Phase 2 as a Hunter — BM or Survival, whichever it shows me. If it can just show me a gear list or upgrade path without me having to already know the game inside and out, that's a win. If it's just another spreadsheet-with-extra-steps, I'll close the tab.

## Their report, written after the visit

### P01: Dana

#### In one paragraph
I came in cold off a Discord link wanting one thing: what to chase in Phase 2 gear-wise on my Hunter. A short faction/race/class/spec wizard got me to a "Ranked Gear" list that answered exactly that, slot by slot, with raid and boss names I actually still remember (Serpentshrine Cavern, Lady Vashj). A Spec Tier List page even confirmed the spec it had defaulted me into (Beast Mastery) was actually good. That's the win. The Simulation tab, on the other hand, buried a single DPS number under paragraphs of theorycrafting jargon I didn't ask for, and there was no way to just accept the whole recommended list at once - I'd have had to click "equip" nineteen times by hand.

#### First impression
Front page reads like a hub for people actively raiding Phase 2 right now: big title, "TBC Classic - Phase 2," and five cards (Character Planner, Raid Composition, Spec Tier Lists, Raids, Professions). My first read was "this is a min-max reference site for a raiding guild," and that a Character Planner would be where I'd plug in my class and get told what to wear. I was right - that's exactly what it turned out to be.

#### What I came for, and what I actually did
I came to find out what gear to chase in Phase 2 on my Hunter. I clicked into Character Planner (step 2), went through the 4-step wizard picking Alliance, Night Elf, Hunter, and accepted the default Beast Mastery spec (steps 3-9). That dropped me into a gear screen with everything empty, so I went to the Ranked Gear tab (step 10), which gave me a #1/#2 ranked item per slot with the raid and boss it drops from. I equipped the top Head and Neck items to see it work (steps 11-12), watched my stats update, scrolled through a few more slots to confirm there wasn't a faster way to fill the rest (step 13), checked the Build tab thinking it might auto-fill the recommended set (step 15, it didn't - it's a save/share/import screen), checked Spec Tier Lists to make sure Beast Mastery wasn't a trap (step 16 - it's S-tier, confirmed), and then poked at Simulation out of curiosity (steps 17-19), which produced a DPS number I couldn't really interpret. I went back to the Gear tab to confirm my two equipped items stuck (steps 20-24).

#### Where I got stuck or confused
- **Step 6/7 (Class step):** the helper text under "Class" read "Only the classes your race can actually be are offered." That's a grammar snarl - I understood it, but it made me pause for a second.
- **Steps 9, 23, 24 (Gear tab, Key Totals box):** there's a line of text that just says "The rail carries all twenty-six." under my stat totals. I have no idea what a "rail" is here or what it's carrying. It reads like debug or placeholder text that got left in. I looked twice (once on the screenshot, once with a plain-text read) and it's really there both times.
- **Steps 13-15:** I expected the Ranked Gear list to have some "equip all" or "apply this build" shortcut, since it's already telling me the exact item per slot. There wasn't one on the list itself, and the Build tab - which sounded like it might do that - turned out to be about saving/sharing/importing a build, not generating one.
- **Steps 17-19 (Simulation):** I expected a number and maybe a one-line verdict. Instead I got a wall of caveats before I even ran it ("Rotation coverage is the standing gap: 5 specs layer their real special attacks on top of auto attacks, and the other 22 are modeled from a single signature ability..."), then a result of "75.9" Estimated DPS followed by an even longer paragraph about pet "damage families," focus bars, and a whole separate "What this estimate misses for your spec" block mentioning Bestial Wrath, Kill Command, and Frenzy. I never got a sense of whether 75.9 was good, bad, or meaningless (it's the latter, since I had no ranged weapon equipped, but that took real reading to work out).
- **Step 21:** I clicked the text "GEAR" to switch tabs and it matched a stray mention of the word inside an unrelated sentence in the sidebar instead of the actual tab button - nothing happened. Had to retarget the actual button by role.

#### What I liked
- The wizard (Faction -> Race -> Class -> Specialization) was fast, and it correctly narrowed race-appropriate classes and class-appropriate specs without me having to know the rules myself.
- Ranked Gear is genuinely the feature I was hoping existed: a plain per-slot list with item name, raid/dungeon, and boss, in vocabulary I still remember even after years away.
- Spec Tier List settled a real doubt I had (whether Beast Mastery is still good) in one glance, and it even tagged my own spec so I didn't have to hunt for it in the list.
- Equipping an item gave instant, visible feedback - the paper doll and the stat totals both updated right away.

#### What I never found, or gave up on
I never found a way to accept the whole ranked-gear build in one action - I stopped after two slots because clicking "equip" seventeen more times wasn't worth it once I already had the information (the ranked list itself told me what I needed). I also never got a real read on my simulated DPS; I gave up trying to parse whether 75.9 meant anything before the session ended.

#### Words or labels I didn't understand
"The rail carries all twenty-six." (Gear tab, Key Totals box) - no idea what this means. In Simulation: "rotation coverage," "signature ability," "damage families span 0.91 to 1.1 upstream," and "Focused Fire" were all used like I should already know them.

#### Three things I would change
1. Fix or remove "The rail carries all twenty-six." on the Gear tab - it reads like leftover placeholder text and undermines trust in the rest of the numbers next to it.
2. Add a one-click "equip the recommended build" action on the Ranked Gear tab (or make the Build tab do this) instead of requiring 19 individual clicks.
3. Lead the Simulation result with one plain sentence (e.g., "this is roughly your DPS with the gear you have; equip a ranged weapon to include Steady Shot") and move the theorycrafting caveats behind a "details" toggle.

#### Would I come back? Would I recommend it, and to whom?
Yes, for the Ranked Gear planner specifically - I'd come back each phase to re-check my slot list. I'd recommend it to the guildmate who sent it, and to other returning players who just want a shopping list, with a heads-up to skip the Simulation tab unless they're into the deep math.

#### Why I stopped
The session was cut off by a technical fault on the observer's side after step 24 (the browser session closed) - not something I chose or that happened in-character. At that point I had just re-read the Gear tab's text output to confirm the odd "The rail carries all twenty-six" line was really there. What I was about to do next was either hover/inspect that line further or equip something in the Ranged slot so the Simulation number would actually include Steady Shot and mean something - and then most likely wrap up, since I'd already gotten the core answer I came for from the Ranked Gear and Spec Tier List pages.

#### Plan versus reality
Plan.md said I give a new site 15-30 seconds and look for my own situation (Hunter, Phase 2) before anything else - that held up exactly: I went straight to Character Planner off the front page. I said I wouldn't make an account for a tool I'm trying out - never got asked for one, which is a point in the site's favor. I said I'd push through one or two confusing screens because a guildmate vouched for it but not more - the 4-step wizard was that "one confusing-ish screen" and it paid off immediately with the Ranked Gear list, which is exactly why I kept going into Simulation afterward instead of bailing. Simulation's wall of text is the kind of thing my plan said would make me leave a site; the only reason I tolerated it here is that I'd already gotten my real answer earlier in the visit and was just curious.
