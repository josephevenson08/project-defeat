## Their plan, written before the visit

### Plan — P08 (Jay, 27, the ten-second skimmer)

- I don't read, I scan. I look at the top-left first — logo, nav, headline — and decide in a couple
  seconds if this is even worth my time.
- I'm looking for the biggest, most obvious button/link on the page and I'll click that before I read
  any body copy. If nothing jumps out, I start losing interest fast.
- I don't trust a site that looks janky or half-built. If it looks like a school project or the design
  is inconsistent, I assume it's not worth my time and bail.
- I'm not going to hunt for a menu or scroll through paragraphs to figure out what something is. If the
  point of the page isn't obvious in the first screen, I'm gone.
- I have zero patience for walls of text, sign-up walls, or anything that makes me think before I've
  even decided if I care.
- I'll give a page maybe 10-20 seconds total unless something specifically hooks me (like, oh hey, that's
  my class/spec).

#### What I'm hoping to get out of this

Honestly not much — someone dropped a link in Twitch chat while I was watching a raid stream, so I'm
just curious what it is. If it's some WoW theorycrafting/sim tool for my Rogue I might poke at it for a
minute. If I can't tell what it does in a few seconds I'm closing the tab and going back to chat.

## Their report, written after the visit

### P08: Jay, 27, the ten-second skimmer

#### In one paragraph
It's a genuinely deep TBC Classic gear/DPS planning tool for a raiding rogue, and the parts built around one big obvious button (the creation wizard, the Ranked Gear "EQUIP" list) worked and felt good. But the plain Gear tab's weapon picker silently failed to equip anything, and the Simulation tab handed me a DPS number that looked flat-out wrong next to a giant wall of caveat text I was never going to read. That combination killed my trust before I found a reason to stay.

#### First impression
Step 1: TBC Classic WoW tool, for Alliance/Horde raiders specifically (Phase 2). Five clean cards — Character Planner, Raid Composition, Spec Tier Lists, Raids, Professions — each with a one-line description, no fluff, no header/nav bar on the landing screen. I could tell what it was in about two seconds. That held up: it really is a raid-planning suite for people who already play this game, not a general explainer.

#### What I came for, and what I actually did
Came for nothing specific — link from Twitch chat, just curious. Clicked into Character Planner (step 2), ran the faction/race/class/spec wizard as a Human Assassination Rogue (steps 2-8), tried to equip a weapon on the Gear tab (steps 9-13, didn't visibly work), switched to Ranked Gear and equipped a helm from its list instead (steps 14-16, worked immediately), then checked the Simulation tab for a DPS number (steps 17-20) and got 42.8 next to a wall of jargon.

#### Where I got stuck or confused
- **Steps 9-13**: opened the Main Hand slot, picked "[141] Fang of Vashj" from the item list. It highlighted blue and the underlying combobox value updated, but the detail panel kept reading "Empty / No stats recorded for this item," and after closing the modal the slot itself still said "Empty" with stats unchanged. I expected clicking an item to equip it on the spot, like every other click on this site. Instead: nothing, no error either. I also tried clicking a "Close" button that didn't exist by that label (step 12) — had to guess the icon was an unlabeled × in the corner.
- **Steps 19-20**: hit "Run Simulation" and got "Estimated DPS: 42.8" for a rogue with raid epics already equipped. That's nowhere near right for a geared rogue in this game. Right below it, a big orange heading ("What this estimate misses for your spec") led into a paragraph naming things like `weaponDamageMultiplier` — implementation detail leaking into visitor-facing copy.
- **Step 8**: the Key Totals box ends with "The rail carries all twenty-six." No idea what that means; reads like leftover debug/placeholder text.

#### What I liked
- The **Ranked Gear** tab (step 14+): numbered #1/#2 items per slot with a big green "EQUIP [ITEM NAME]" button that worked instantly — Agility/Stamina updated the moment I clicked (step 15).
- The wizard's last button relabels itself to say exactly what you're about to get — "Play as Human Assassination Rogue" (step 7).
- The front-page cards (step 1) — short, specific, no marketing filler.

#### What I never found, or gave up on
Never finished even one full pass of the Ranked Gear list (step 16) — it's long, one slot at a time. Never opened Raid Composition, Spec Tier Lists, Raids, or Professions. Never determined if the Gear tab's item picker works for any slot at all.

#### Words or labels I didn't understand
- "The rail carries all twenty-six." (Key Totals box, step 8)
- "Rotation coverage is the standing gap" / "5 specs layer their real special attacks... the other 22 are modeled from a single signature ability" (Simulation intro, step 18)
- `weaponDamageMultiplier` (step 20) — a variable name in visitor-facing text
- "Needs source/rank verification before treating as final" (steps 14/16) — odd thing to show a visitor rather than a site admin

#### Three things I would change
1. Fix the Gear-tab weapon/item picker so selecting an item actually equips it, or show a visible loading/error state instead of silently doing nothing.
2. Cut the Simulation tab's explanation to one line ("early estimate, numbers may run low") and move engineering caveats out of the main flow.
3. Clean up leftover strings ("The rail carries all twenty-six.") and the garbled Race-step copy ("Only the classes your race can actually be are offered.").

#### Would I come back? Would I recommend it, and to whom?
Only if actually gearing a raid rogue and specifically wanting the Ranked Gear list — that part worked and I'd trust it more than the simulator. Wouldn't recommend it off a casual link the way I found it; would recommend it to guildies doing real BiS planning, with a warning that the DPS sim looks unreliable right now.

#### Why I stopped
The DPS number looked broken and was wrapped in more text than I'd ever read to understand why. Combined with the weapon picker doing nothing earlier, that used up my patience for a link I'd clicked out of pure curiosity. Nothing was pulling me toward one more click, so I stopped (step 21).

#### Plan versus reality
Matched plan.md closely: scanned top-left first, clicked the biggest/most obvious thing at every step (first card, highlighted defaults, green Equip buttons), never read a paragraph start to finish. One thing plan.md didn't predict: the wizard's one-obvious-button-per-step design pulled me four steps deep before I'd consciously decided to invest time — further than a true 10-second skim usually gets. What ended the visit was exactly what I predicted: something looking broken/untrustworthy with no payoff in sight.

#### Step 4: final message

1. Verdict: A real, fairly deep TBC raid-planning tool, but rough edges (a dead-feeling gear picker, a suspiciously tiny DPS number) cost it my trust before it earned more of my time.
2. Biggest problem: picking a weapon in the Gear tab's item list (step 9-13) highlighted the item but never equipped it — panel stayed "Empty," stats never moved.
3. Best thing: the Ranked Gear tab's green "EQUIP [ITEM]" buttons (step 14-15) — one click, instant stat update, exactly the feedback I wanted.
4. Actions taken: 21 steps; stopped because the Simulation tab's "42.8 DPS" result looked broken and sat under a wall of text I wasn't going to read, and I had no goal pulling me further.
