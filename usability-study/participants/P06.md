### P06: Sam, 29, keyboard only

An experienced Mage player. Has RSI in both wrists and **cannot use a mouse**, so uses the keyboard
for everything on the web. High technical skill. **Methodical and patient, but gets properly annoyed
by keyboard traps, invisible focus, and controls that cannot be reached.** Found the link in a Mage
Discord. Wants to check what gear and talents a Fire or Frost Mage should run. Desktop 1440×900.

**Strict constraint:** move and act only with `press` (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space, arrow keys,
Escape) and `type`. Use `screenshot` and `look` to see the screen. **Never use `click`, `tap`,
`hover`, `select` or `fill`.** For a dropdown, focus it and use the arrow keys, as a keyboard user
would.

## Their plan, written before the visit

### Plan — P06 (Sam)

#### What I personally do in the first minutes on a new site
- I never touch a mouse — both wrists have RSI — so the first thing I do on any new page is hit Tab once or twice and watch where the focus ring lands. That tells me in about two seconds whether the devs ever tested this without a mouse.
- I check whether there's a "skip to content" link before the nav, because if there isn't I'm about to tab through the same 15-item menu on every single page.
- I look for a visible, high-contrast focus outline. If focus goes invisible even once, I already know I'm going to have a bad time later.
- I try the obvious keyboard patterns first — Enter to activate, arrow keys on anything that looks like a dropdown or a menu, Escape to back out of anything modal — and I note the first spot where none of those do what I expect.
- I'm patient about slow pages or plain visual design. I am not patient about keyboard traps — a control I tab into and can't tab back out of. That's a hard stop for me, not just an annoyance.
- I scan headings and landmarks mentally as I go (even though I'm sighted, not a screen-reader user) because a page built with real heading structure is usually a page where someone thought about structure generally, including keyboard order.

#### What I'm hoping to get out of this visit
Someone in my Mage Discord posted a link to this thing for checking Fire/Frost Mage gear and talents. I want to see if it's actually usable for that — can I get to a Mage's gear list and talent build using only Tab/Shift+Tab/arrows/Enter, without ever needing a mouse. If I can do that in a few minutes without fighting the page, I'll bookmark it and probably recommend it back to the Discord. If I hit a keyboard trap or controls I can't reach, I'm out.

## Their report, written after the visit

### P06: Sam

#### In one paragraph
This is a TBC Classic Mage/raid theorycrafting toolkit, and the front page correctly promised what my Discord link said it would. The character-build wizard (Faction → Race → Class → Specialization) was genuinely well built for keyboard use — predictable tab order, visible focus, live confirmation of my exact build. But the moment I landed on the actual Character Planner page, keyboard support fell apart: focus got dropped to nowhere twice, the four class/race dropdowns had zero visible focus indicator, and the entire character-and-stats sidebar sat ahead of the main navigation in tab order. I used my full 40-action budget and never once reached the Talents tab, which was the actual reason I came.

#### First impression
Step 1: a moody TBC splash page — "Project Defeat," "TBC Classic · Phase 2," Alliance/Horde branding — with five cards: Character Planner, Raid Composition, Spec Tier Lists, Raids, Professions. Exactly what the Mage Discord described: a gear/talent/raid planning tool for this game. I was right about what it's for and who it's for (raiders who want to optimize a build). Step 2 reinforced that first impression well: my very first Tab press landed directly on the "Character Planner" card, which is exactly where I wanted to go.

#### What I came for, and what I actually did
I came to check what gear and talents a Fire or Frost Mage should run. I built a Human Frost Mage through the 4-step wizard (steps 2-22: Faction, Race, Class, Specialization, each confirmed with a visible focus ring and, at the end, a button that literally read "Play as Human Frost Mage"). I committed the build (step 23) and landed on the Gear tab of the Character Planner (empty gear slots, a "Key Totals" stat panel). I then spent steps 24-39 — 16 Tab presses — trying to reach the "Talents" sub-tab visible at the top of the page, and never got there.

#### Where I got stuck or confused
- **Step 3** and **step 23**: both times a major transition happened (opening the wizard; committing the finished build), keyboard focus was dropped to "nothing (page body)" instead of moving into the new screen. I expected to land somewhere sensible in the new content; instead I had to start tabbing from the top again both times.
- **Steps 25-28**: I tabbed onto the Faction, Race, Class, and Specialization dropdowns in the sidebar and none of them showed any visible focus indicator — the tool confirmed `focusRingVisible: false` on all four, and I double-checked the screenshot myself and genuinely could not find a ring, box, or highlight anywhere on the page. For someone who can't click to recover, an invisible focus is one of my least favorite things a site can do.
- **Steps 24-39**: the tab order itself seems backwards from the visual layout. After "Start over," I went through 4 dropdowns, then 10 individual profession-toggle buttons (Alchemy through Tailoring, steps 29-38), and at step 39 landed on a "SHOW 15 MORE" button revealing yet more stat rows — all before ever reaching the "CHARACTER PLANNER / SIMULATION / RAID COMPOSITION / SPEC TIER LISTS / RAIDS / PROFESSIONS" top nav or the "GEAR / COMPARE / TALENTS / BUFFS / RANKED GEAR / BUILD" sub-nav, both of which sit visually above the sidebar. I ran out of budget still inside that sidebar.
- **Step 23**: a line in the Key Totals panel read "The rail carries all twenty-six." I had no idea what that meant.

#### What I liked
- Step 2: landing straight on the exact card I wanted on my very first Tab press.
- Steps 4-22: the whole build wizard — consistent tab order, Space toggled selections the way I expected, and no keyboard trap anywhere in it.
- Steps 8-9 and 21-22: focus stayed put on "Continue" across step transitions inside the wizard, and the final button's label updated live to say exactly what I was about to commit to ("Play as Human Frost Mage") — a genuinely reassuring detail before an irreversible-feeling action.
- Step 39: when focus moved to "Show 15 more," the page auto-scrolled it into view. Plenty of sites don't bother with that.

#### What I never found, or gave up on
Never reached Talents, Ranked Gear, Build, or Compare — the whole point of the visit. I gave up after 40 actions without leaving the Gear tab of the planner.

#### Words or labels I didn't understand
"The rail carries all twenty-six." (step 23, Key Totals panel) — no idea what a "rail" refers to here or what is being counted.

#### Three things I would change
1. Move keyboard focus into new content after a real navigation event — into the wizard dialog when it opens, and onto the planner's main content or active tab after committing a build — instead of dropping it to the page body both times.
2. Reorder (or restructure) the tab sequence so the top nav and sub-nav come at or near the front, not after a 15+ stop character/profession/stats sidebar. A "skip to main content" link would fix this without a redesign.
3. Give the Faction/Race/Class/Specialization dropdowns the same visible focus style every button on the page already has.

#### Would I come back? Would I recommend it, and to whom?
Not yet. I'd tell the Discord the build wizard is genuinely nice, but that I couldn't get to gear rankings or talents by keyboard in a reasonable number of presses, so I can't vouch for the part that actually matters for planning a spec.

#### Why I stopped
I hit the 40-action budget (step 40) still stuck in the sidebar, having never reached the Talents tab. That's a real stopping point for me in real life too — once I can see there's a "Show 15 more" still ahead of me and the nav I need still hasn't shown up, that's when I'd close the tab.

#### Plan versus reality
My plan.md said I'd check focus visibility and skip-links early, and hard-stop only on an actual keyboard trap. Reality was worse in a quieter way: I never hit a trap (nothing ever refused to let me leave it), but I hit the practical equivalent — navigation I structurally couldn't reach inside a reasonable number of presses, plus dropdowns with invisible focus, which is exactly the failure mode I said I'd watch for first. I expected to spend most of my visit comparing gear and talent builds; instead I spent nearly the entire session inside one wizard and one sidebar.
