## Their plan, written before the visit

### Plan — P12 Rafael

#### What I do first minutes on new site
- I don't read, I look for icons and pictures first. Words come after.
- I look for big title top, and I look for boss names or class names I already know. If I see "Serpentshrine" or a boss icon, I know I'm in right place.
- I scroll fast one time top to bottom, just to see how big the page is, before I read anything slow.
- If sentence is long or have hard word, I skip it. I look for the short version, numbers, bullet list.
- If I don't find what I want in like 1-2 minutes, I get annoyed and I think about leaving.
- I like when icon + text together, this way if I don't know the word I still understand from icon.

#### What I hope to get from this visit
- I want to learn about the raid bosses in Serpentshrine Cavern before my first run. Boss names, maybe what they do (abilities), so I don't look bad in front of my guild.
- I hope is easy to find, like click "Serpentshrine Cavern" and see list of bosses with pictures.
- I hope I don't need read big paragraph of text for each boss, just key point.

## Their report, written after the visit

### P12: Rafael

#### In one paragraph
I came to learn the Serpentshrine Cavern bosses before my first run. I found the raid fast (step 2-3), the loot lists are nice with icons and numbers (step 5), but nobody tells me what the boss actually does in the fight. Only loot, quest steps, and a big DPS calculator. I like the site for gear, but not for what I wanted today.

#### First impression
Big fantasy banner, gold title "Project Defeat", "TBC Classic - Phase 2" (step 1). I think: ok, this is tool for WoW TBC player, like Wowhead but smaller. Five cards: Character Planner, Raid Composition, Spec Tier Lists, Raids, Professions. I think this is for people who already play and want make character stronger, not really for learn the game. I was right about "for TBC player" but wrong that I would find boss fight info — this is gear/math tool, not story or strategy guide.

#### What I came for, and what I actually did
I came for: learn about Serpentshrine Cavern bosses before first run.
I actually did: Home -> Raids card (step 2) -> Serpentshrine Cavern (step 3) -> opened loot list for Hydross the Unstable (step 5) -> scrolled all 6 boss + trash (step 4, 8, 9, 14) -> read Attunement tab quest chain (step 11-12) -> tried click boss name direct, only toggle loot (step 15) -> peek Simulation page (step 16-17) -> peek Character Planner wizard step 1 (step 18) -> cancel and stop (step 19-20).

#### Where I got stuck or confused
- Step 3-4: Big paragraph under "Serpentshrine Cavern" title is long, hard for me, so I skip it. But after, I realize this paragraph was the ONLY place with any boss info at all ("Hydross needs frost-resistance tank", "Lady Vashj is three-phase fight"). I skip the one part that had what I want.
- Step 4, 8, 9: every boss box (Hydross, Lurker, Leotheras, Karathress, Morogrim, Lady Vashj) shows big black empty box where I expect picture. The raid-select page one step before (step 2) had nice big art for each raid, so I expect same for boss. Feels broken.
- Step 15: I click direct on "Lady Vashj" name, thinking maybe this opens boss page. It only open/close the same loot list. No boss page exist.
- Step 16-17: I click "Simulation" in menu and page show me the bottom first, I have to scroll up myself to read from top. Small thing but strange.
- Step 11-12: Attunement text also long, words like "front-loads the easy loot" I don't really understand first time.

#### What I liked
- Home page cards short and clear, easy scan (step 1).
- Raid picker page: big picture for each raid, I recognize Serpentshrine Cavern by the spider-lady art immediately, don't even need read much (step 2).
- Loot list: icon + name + number stats, this is very good for me, I don't need read much, I understand from numbers (step 5).
- Character Planner start: big simple buttons Alliance / Horde, "Step 1 of 4", very clear (step 18).

#### What I never found, or gave up on
I never found any text about what a boss does — abilities, what to watch, how not to die. I check Raids section (loot + attunement) and I check Simulation and start of Character Planner. Nothing. I gave up after that.

#### Words or labels I didn't understand
"resistance-gear raid", "three-phase execution fight", "front-loads the easy loot" (step 3), "attack-table/spell-table simulation", "Rotation coverage is the standing gap", "signature ability" (step 17). Small label "LOOT TABLES" above "RAIDS" on home page (step 1) I didn't notice until the end — if I saw this small word first, maybe I understand faster this section is only items.

#### Three things I would change
1. Fix the black empty boxes on every boss card — put the boss picture like the raid picker page has.
2. Add small boss info — even just 3-4 bullet with icon, like "casts big frost attack, don't stand in water" — not just loot.
3. Make that one important paragraph (step 3) into short bullets, not long sentence, because right now the only boss hint is buried there and people like me skip it.

#### Would I come back? Would I recommend it, and to myself?
Maybe, later, when I already build my character and need check gear or loot. I would recommend to guild for the gear planner, but I tell them "don't look here for boss guide, still need Wowhead or ask guild for that."

#### Why I stopped
I check the main places (Raids loot, Attunement, Simulation, Character Planner start) and boss ability info is not anywhere. I am tired from the long sentences and I already know this site not have what I need today, so no reason to keep going.

#### Plan versus reality
My plan.md say I scan for icon/picture and known names first — that's true, I found Serpentshrine Cavern fast by the name and picture (step 2). I hoped click raid and see bosses with picture and short point — I got bosses list but no picture (bug) and no short strategy point, only loot. So half my hope came true.
